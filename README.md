# 🌴 Welcome to Leonida!

Hey, I'm **Satyajit Beura**. Student, space nerd, developer, and apparently the kind of person who schedules a portfolio launch around a video game.

Welcome to **Leonida**, my little corner of the internet, inspired by GTA VI.

I could've made a normal portfolio. I chose palm trees, neon lights, and questionable levels of dedication instead.

## 🎮 Why Leonida?

GTA VI is scheduled for **19 November 2026**. So naturally, I launched this portfolio at **12 AM on 19 October**, exactly one month before the game.

Was this necessary? No.

Would I do it again? Absolutely.

And before anyone asks, **RDR2 is still my favourite game.** Arthur Morgan, you have some competition. Maybe.
