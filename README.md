# 🌴 Welcome to Leonida

Hi, I'm **Satyajit Beura**. This is my new personal portfolio: a cinematic, game-inspired home for my projects, experiments, achievements, and the occasional idea that refuses to leave my brain alone.

It is not trying to be another quiet page that says *“Hello, I'm a developer”* above three cards and a button. I wanted it to feel like entering a world: missions, atmosphere, satisfying interactions, sounds, and a little bit of chaos. You know, the normal amount of effort people put into a personal website. Completely reasonable.

> **The premise:** a portfolio with GTA-style energy, built by a fan who has clearly spent too long thinking about video games and interface design.

## 🚨 The one-month-before-GTA-VI story

This repository was created at **exactly 12:00 AM IST on 19 October 2026**.

Why that moment? Because *Grand Theft Auto VI* is currently scheduled for **19 November 2026**. That makes the repository's birth exactly **one calendar month before the announced release date**. For once, the calendar has excellent timing.

I'm a **massive GTA VI fan**. GTA V gave us Los Santos, endless things to do, and the ability to lose an entire evening by deciding to “just drive around for five minutes.” Five minutes, apparently measured on a planet with very unusual physics.

And then there's **Red Dead Redemption 2**. As of writing this, RDR2 is my favourite game I've played so far. Arthur Morgan has set a truly unfair standard for storytelling, characters, music, and emotional damage delivered by pixels.

My expectation? **GTA VI is coming for that throne.** Will it take it? I hope so. But I'll let the actual game make its case. Hype is not a review, even when the hype has been living rent-free in my head for years.

And so, in the grand tradition of the internet:

## 🎮 What is Leonida?

**Leonida is my new portfolio**, using a cinematic, open-world-game-inspired interface to introduce the person behind the projects.

- **A game-like experience** — mission-style navigation, status panels, and a world to explore instead of a plain list of links.
- **Projects and experiments** — the things I build, test, learn from, and occasionally rebuild because apparently one version was not enough.
- **Motion and atmosphere** — smooth transitions, interface animations, neon accents, and custom sound effects where they make sense.
- **Made for phones, too** — because a portfolio that only behaves on a giant desktop monitor is basically a very expensive screenshot.
- **A personal story** — I'm interested in science, space, technology, and building useful things. The game theme is the wrapping; curiosity is the actual package.

The experience is being built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, with a focus on a self-contained, easy-to-open web experience.
